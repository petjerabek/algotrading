The Organisers may act through authorised members of the organising team. They are authorised to manage the event, issue instructions to participants, monitor compliance with these Rules and decide on matters concerning participation and the conduct of the competition.

2. Eligibility
A person may participate in the competition if, on the first day of the event, they:

are at least 18 years old; and
are enrolled in a bachelor's or master's degree programme at a university
or are a secondary-school student.
Doctoral students may participate in the competition on the condition that they are not employed full-time outside of academia.

A person whose studies in an otherwise eligible study programme have been officially suspended may also participate. A graduate who has already completed their studies and is not currently enrolled in another eligible study programme may not participate in the competition.

Participants may be asked to provide reasonable evidence of their age, current or suspended studies and compliance with the eligibility requirements. The Organisers may refuse or cancel the participation of anyone who fails to provide the required documents or supplies false, incomplete or misleading information during registration.

Each participant must:

provide truthful, complete and accurate information during registration;
accept and comply with these Official Rules and the Technical Rules;
comply with the applicable safety and fire-protection rules of Charles University;
have access to the eduroam network or arrange their own internet connection (wifi).
The competition is not open to anyone who participated in its development, testing or administration, or who had access to non-public materials or infrastructure used for evaluation.

3. Teams and registration
Participants compete in teams of two to four members. Each participant may belong to only one team.

If the number of eligible applications exceeds the event capacity, complete teams with more members will be given priority over smaller teams. Among eligible teams of the same size, priority will be determined by the time at which the complete application was received.

Registration alone does not guarantee participation. A team is accepted into the competition only when it receives confirmation of acceptance from the Organisers.

A team's composition becomes final when its acceptance is confirmed. Any subsequent substitution, addition or removal of a team member, as well as any transfer of a participant between teams, requires the Organisers' prior approval. A team wishing to request a change must contact the Organisers directly. The Organisers may refuse a change if it could affect participant eligibility, event capacity or organisation, or the fairness of the competition.

A breach of the Rules by one team member may have consequences for the entire team, including its disqualification.

4. In-person participation and access to the building
The event is held exclusively in person. Remote participation is not permitted.

Participants may enter only the rooms and areas designated for the event. Entry to computer laboratories, staff-only areas and other non-public or restricted parts of the building is prohibited without express permission.

Access to the event is restricted to registered participants, the Organisers, authorised members of the organising team and invited guests. Participants must not provide their registration details, access cards or any other means of entering the building to another person.

Participants should work primarily at the place assigned to their team.

No designated sleeping areas or other accommodation facilities will be available in the building. Participants may leave the building and rest elsewhere. Anyone planning to leave the venue for an extended period must notify one of the organisers present at the venue before leaving.

Leaving the venue does not entitle a participant to compete remotely or use assistance from anyone who is not a member of their team.

The exact event times, check-in time, opening briefing, individual parts of the competition and announcement of the results will be communicated to accepted participants in advance.

5. Venue rules, safety and conduct
Participants must comply with all safety, fire-protection, hygiene and operating regulations applicable at the venue, as well as instructions from the Organisers, faculty staff, security personnel and members of the integrated rescue system.

In particular, participants must:

behave responsibly and not create a risk to themselves or others;
follow all instructions concerning emergencies and evacuation;
treat furniture, equipment and other property with care;
keep the building and its immediate surroundings tidy;
eat and drink only in designated areas;
not obstruct escape routes, corridors, fire-safety equipment or access routes;
immediately report to the Organisers any accident, injury, property damage, fire, safety risk or other serious incident.
Quiet hours apply from 22:00 to 6:00. During this period, playing recorded music or engaging in any other unnecessarily noisy conduct is prohibited.

Smoking is prohibited throughout the building. Smoking is permitted only in the designated outdoor area.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, violence, intimidation and deliberately disruptive or dangerous conduct will not be tolerated. Participants must treat other participants, the Organisers, venue staff and guests with respect.

The Organisers may require a participant to leave the venue immediately if this is reasonably necessary to protect health, safety, property, the orderly operation of the building or other participants.

6. Technical Rules
At the beginning of the hackathon, the Organisers will publish detailed Technical Rules and instructions governing the competition format, permitted resources and tools, the evaluation process, calculation of results, ranking, tie-breaking rules and technical limitations. These rules and instructions will also be explained to all teams during the opening briefing.

The final competition solution must be submitted in Python. When researching, preparing and developing their solution, teams may use any tools and information sources, including other programming languages, artificial intelligence tools, specialist literature and publicly available online resources, unless the Technical Rules provide otherwise.

During the competition, participants may not publicly post questions concerning the task or solution on online forums or in discussion groups, or seek advice or assistance from any specific person outside their team, including acquaintances.

These Official Rules take precedence in organisational, legal, eligibility and disciplinary matters and in matters concerning prizes. The Technical Rules take precedence in matters concerning the technical conduct of the competition, evaluation and calculation of results.

The Technical Rules and any clarifications concerning the competition will be provided to all teams on equal terms.

7. Fair conduct of the competition
Teams must work independently of all other teams.

In particular, it is prohibited to:

collaborate with another team on a competition solution;
share competition-related knowledge, code, models, strategies or methods between teams;
attempt to obtain, copy or use another team's solution or non-public knowledge;
accept unauthorised assistance from anyone who is not a team member;
interfere with another team's work;
attempt to manipulate the solution submission or evaluation system, circumvent its rules, disrupt its operation or gain unauthorised access to it;
submit malicious code;
conceal a breach of the Official Rules or the Technical Rules.
Purely technical assistance unrelated to the substance of a competition solution, such as help with the official interface or submission method, is permitted. However, the organising team remains the primary point of contact for technical questions.

If an explanation or resolution of a technical issue could also materially assist other teams, the Organisers may communicate it to all teams.

8. Evaluation and final results
Submitted solutions will be evaluated in accordance with the Technical Rules.

All leaderboards and results displayed during the event are preliminary. The Organisers will confirm the final ranking after verifying the validity and reproducibility of the highest-ranked solutions and their compliance with the competition rules.

The Organisers are entitled to inspect and rerun submitted solutions for verification purposes. A result achieved through a breach of the Rules, manipulation, an invalid solution or a serious evaluation error does not give rise to an entitlement to a prize.

If a technical or organisational issue materially affects the fairness of the competition, the Organisers may take reasonable corrective action. In particular, they may suspend or extend part of the competition, repeat the evaluation or disregard the affected result. Teams affected under comparable conditions will be treated equally.

The Organisers' confirmation of the final ranking is final, subject to the disqualification provisions below.

9. Disqualification
The Organisers may disqualify a participant or team if they have reasonable grounds to suspect a breach of these Official Rules or the Technical Rules.

Where reasonably possible in the circumstances, the team concerned will be given an opportunity to comment before a final decision is issued.

In serious or clear-cut cases, particularly an attempt to interfere with the evaluation system, submission of malicious code, violent conduct, serious harassment or conduct presenting an immediate safety risk, the Organisers may remove or disqualify a participant or team immediately and without prior notice.

A disqualified team forfeits any entitlement to a prize. If disqualification occurs during the event, the team must cease participating in the competition and may be required to leave the venue.

Disqualification is decided by the Organisers, whose decision is final.

10. Prizes
The gross prize amounts are as follows:

1st place: CZK 100,000;
2nd place: CZK 60,000;
3rd place: CZK 40,000.
The stated amounts are gross amounts before the deduction of any taxes or other withholdings required by law.

A prize is awarded to a team as a whole. Before it is paid, each participant on the team must provide confirmation that they met the student-status requirement at the time of the competition. Each winning team must also provide Qminers with written instructions for allocating the prize among the individual team members. The chosen allocation must be confirmed by all team members.

Each team member will be paid their allocated share of the prize separately by bank transfer.

No team member may claim from Qminers an amount exceeding the share confirmed in that team's written instructions.

11. Payment and taxation of prizes
Qminers will pay the individual shares of a prize within 30 days after:

the final results have been confirmed;
it has received all required payment, identification and tax information from the relevant team members;
any investigation concerning the team's eligibility or the result achieved has been concluded.
Qminers may withhold payment until it has received all information that may reasonably be required to make the payment, verify identity and comply with tax obligations.

Qminers will make all tax withholdings required by applicable law. Each winner remains responsible for meeting their own tax obligations, including any obligations arising outside the Czech Republic.

Any bank charges imposed by the winner's bank or arising from the use of a foreign bank account are borne by the payment recipient.

If a member of a winning team fails to provide the required information within a reasonable period specified by Qminers, payment of their share may be deferred until the required information is provided.

12. Ownership and use of submitted solutions
Teams retain ownership of the original code, ideas and other materials they create and submit.

By submitting a solution, each team grants the Organisers a royalty-free, non-exclusive right to store, run, reproduce and review the solution to the extent reasonably necessary to organise, evaluate, verify and document the competition.

Each team confirms that it is entitled to submit its solution and that, to the best of its knowledge, the solution does not infringe another person's rights. Third-party and open-source materials may be used only in accordance with the Technical Rules and the applicable licence terms.

The Organisers may publish the names and rankings of the winning teams and a general description of their solutions.

Participants' source code and confidential technical details will not be published without a separate agreement with the team concerned.

13. Photography and promotion
Photographs and video recordings may be made during the event for documentation and promotional purposes. These may include photographs and video recordings of competitors and winning teams.

The Organisers may publish these materials on their websites, social-media accounts and in other communications concerning the event or the Organisers' activities.

The names of individual participants will not be published in connection with these photographs or video recordings unless the participant concerned gives separate consent.

A participant who does not wish to appear in photographs or video recordings must inform the Organisers during check-in. They will receive a visibly distinct identification badge or coloured wristband, which they must keep visible throughout the event. The Organisers will take reasonable measures to ensure that participants bearing this identification do not appear in published materials.

Participants must also reasonably cooperate with photographers and organisers and comply with any further instructions concerning the making of recordings.

14. Personal data
Matfyzák, spolek studentů a přátel MFF UK, and Qminers, s.r.o., act as joint controllers of personal data processed in connection with the organisation and conduct of the event.

The joint controllers process participants' personal data primarily for the following purposes:

participant registration and communication;
verification of compliance with the eligibility requirements;
event organisation, access control and safety;
administration and evaluation of the competition;
confirmation and publication of the results;
payment and taxation of prizes;
documentation and promotion of the event;
establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
The data processed may include identification and contact details, information about age and studies, team membership, data concerning participation in the competition, submitted solutions and, for winners, bank, identification and tax information. Photographs and video recordings may also be processed in accordance with Section 13.

Further information about the joint controllers, the legal bases for processing, the allocation of their responsibilities, data recipients, data-retention periods and participants' rights will be provided in the Privacy Notice available at arena.qminers.com.

Participants may exercise their data-protection rights through the official contact channel specified at arena.qminers.com, regardless of which joint controller processes the relevant data.

Provision of the data required to verify eligibility, administer the competition or pay a prize is mandatory. Without this data, participation in the competition or payment of a prize may not be possible.

Registration for the competition does not in itself constitute consent to receive other commercial or marketing communications. The Organisers reserve the right to contact participants to invite them to another competition of a similar type.

15. Personal belongings, damage and liability
Participants are responsible for looking after their personal belongings. To the extent permitted by law, neither the Organisers nor the venue provider are liable for the loss of or damage to personal belongings left unattended.

Each participant is liable, in accordance with applicable law, for any damage they cause to the venue, its equipment or another person.

Any damage must be reported to the Organisers immediately. The participant responsible must provide reasonable cooperation in documenting and resolving the incident.

Nothing in these Rules excludes or limits any liability that cannot be excluded or limited under applicable law.

16. Amendments to the Rules, suspension and cancellation of the event
The Organisers may amend these Rules where reasonably necessary to:

correct an error or ambiguity;
ensure compliance with applicable law or binding requirements of the venue provider;
protect health, safety or property;
address fraud, misconduct or a safety risk;
preserve equal and fair conditions for the competition;
address an unforeseen organisational or technical issue.
Any material amendment will be communicated to all affected participants. Amendments will not be applied retroactively if this would unfairly disadvantage a participant, unless retroactive application is necessary to remedy an invalid or unfair result.

The Organisers may suspend, postpone or cancel the event for serious reasons. Such reasons may include unavailability of the venue, an emergency, a serious safety risk or circumstances beyond the Organisers' reasonable control.

17. Final provisions
These Rules are governed by the laws of the Czech Republic.

Questions concerning the event or these Rules may be submitted through the official contact channel specified at arena.qminers.com.

The Rules will be published in Czech and English. In the event of any conflict between the two versions, the Czech version prevails.

By participating in the event, each participant confirms that they have read, understood and agreed to these Official Rules and the Technical Rules.
